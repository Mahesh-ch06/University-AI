### Who gets which UG merit scholarship (2027-28)

Only ONE scholarship applies: the best one among the student's scores. Scholarships are not added together.
The standard fee (no scholarship) is for Intermediate 55 to 59.9% in B.Tech CSE and ECE, and 50 to 59.9% in other programs.

| Scholarship on tuition | Intermediate / State Board % | CBSE / ICSE % | JEE Main percentile | TG / AP EAPCET rank | IB Diploma score (out of 45) |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| **10%** | 60 – 64.9% | 60 – 64.9% | 50 – 59.9 %ile | not applicable | not applicable |
| **15%** | 65 – 69.9% | 65 – 69.9% | 60 – 69.9 %ile | not applicable | not applicable |
| **20%** | 70 – 79.9% | 70 – 79.9% | 70 – 79.9 %ile | not applicable | 24 – 29 |
| **25%** | 80 – 91.9% | 80 – 89.9% | 80 – 89.9 %ile | 8,001 – 20,000 | 30 – 33 |
| **30%** | 92 – 97.9% | 90 – 94.9% | 90 – 94.5 %ile | 2,001 – 8,000 | 34 – 37 |
| **40%** | 98% and above | 95% and above | 95 %ile and above | 1 – 2,000 | 38 and above |

- An EAPCET rank above 20,000 does not earn a rank-based scholarship. The student's Intermediate, CBSE/ICSE, JEE or IB score may still earn one.
- The fee circular's IB ranges overlap (30-33 under 25%, 30-37 under 30%, 35/45+ under 40%). The lower figure is applied here; the Admissions Office confirms the exact slab for IB scores 30 to 37.
