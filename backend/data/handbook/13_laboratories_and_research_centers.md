# SR University — Laboratories, Research Centers & Infrastructure

> Verified institutional documentation covering SR University laboratories, research centers, incubator maker spaces, and departmental facilities.

---

## 1. How Many Labs Are There in SR University?

SR University does not operate with a single static count for all laboratories because facilities are distributed across three distinct levels of infrastructure:

1. **11 Interdisciplinary Specialized Research Centers** with dedicated advanced research laboratories.
2. **SR Innovation Exchange (SRiX)** hosting **14+ maker spaces**, an **IoT & ESDM lab**, and a **3D rapid prototyping facility**.
3. **Departmental Academic Laboratories across 6 Schools**: Every department maintains multiple dedicated teaching and practical coursework laboratories.

---

## 2. 11 Specialized Research Centers & Associated Labs

SR University houses 11 interdisciplinary research centers focused on high-impact research, patents, and industry collaborations:

1. **Center for AI & Deep Learning (CAIDL)**:
   - High-performance GPU computing clusters, deep neural network training environments, and computer vision systems supporting AI/ML research.
2. **Center for Creative Cognition (CCC)**:
   - Houses 6 distinct cutting-edge laboratories:
     * *Ideation Lab*
     * *Eye-tracking Lab*
     * *EEG (Electroencephalography) Lab*
     * *Computational Neuroscience Lab*
     * *Driving Simulator Lab*
     * *Psychometric Testing Lab*
3. **Center for Embedded Systems & IoT (EIoT)**:
   - High-end computing systems, Raspberry Pi development platforms, ARM Cortex M4 development boards, and wireless sensor networking equipment.
4. **Center for Emerging Energy Technologies (CEET)**:
   - Houses three core research setups:
     * *Advanced Simulation Lab* (equipped with LabVIEW Academy tools)
     * *Energy Lab*
     * *Electric Drives Lab* (EV powertrain and electric mobility research)
5. **Center for Materials & Manufacturing (CMM)**:
   - *Product Design Studio*
   - *CAD/CAM Lab*
   - *Makers Space* equipped with CNC machinery, additive manufacturing (3D printing), and material testing apparatus.
6. **Center for Construction Methods and Materials (CCMM)**:
   - Concrete Technology Lab
   - Triaxial Testing Machine
   - Digital Consolidation Apparatus
   - Automatic Asphalt Compactor
   - Ultrasonic Concrete Tester
   - Asphalt Mixer
7. **Centre for Nanomaterials**:
   - Advanced nanomaterial synthesis, characterization setups, and high-resolution microscopy for nanoscience research.
8. **Center for Informetrics & Statistics**:
   - Computational data modeling, statistical informatics, and research analytics.
9. **Center for Experiential Learning**:
   - Multi-disciplinary studios for hands-on student projects, capstones, and experiential coursework.
10. **Collaboratory for Social Innovation**:
    - Community engineering, rural technology interventions, and social impact development labs.
11. **Center for Design**:
    - Industrial design studios, ergonomics modeling, and human-centered design workshops.

## 3. SR Innovation Exchange (SRiX) — Product Development & Maker Spaces

SRiX is the on-campus Technology Business Incubator (TBI) supported by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India:

* **14+ Maker Spaces** and open innovation project studios.
* **IoT & ESDM Lab** (Electronic System Design & Manufacturing).
* **3D Prototyping & Rapid Prototyping Facility** (Maker Space, 3D printers, laser cutters).
* **Design Thinking Studio** for ideation and product validation.
* Supported 171+ startups with ₹76+ Crore external funding.

## 5. Lab Access, Timings & Safety Guidelines

* **Working Hours**: Laboratories are open during university instructional hours and scheduled practical sessions. Extended access is provided for research scholars and students undertaking capstone or incubator projects.
* **Connectivity**: The entire campus—including academic blocks, research centers, laboratories, library, and hostels—is 100% Wi-Fi enabled.
* **Lab Assistance**: Ph.D. research scholars provide up to 8 hours per week of laboratory and teaching assistance.
* **Safety Protocols**: All students must follow standard safety procedures (lab coats, safety goggles where applicable, proper equipment handling, and faculty/technician supervision).
