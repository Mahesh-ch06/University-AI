# SR University — Events, Clubs, Hostel Life & Food (approved 24 Nov 2026)

Sources: sru.edu.in Events, Campus Life and Clubs & Organisations pages (November 2026); the SR University
admissions presentation (30 Oct 2026); the SR University hostel rules document; the official SR University
YouTube channel. Approved by the SR University admissions team on 24 November 2026.

## Events and fests (50+ events every year)
* **Sparkrill (also written Sparkill)** — SR University's annual day and biggest cultural festival: performances,
  live music by guest artists (e.g. Ram Miriyala at Sparkrill '24) and the fashion ramp walk. Sparkill '27 is on
  26 January 2027.
* **Lumina — Hostel Night (Hostel Hawa)** — the hostellers' own annual celebration, with separate Girls' and Boys'
  Hostel Nights: music, dance and memories with friends (LUMINA 2025).
* **Navadhara — Talent & Culture** — a celebration of talent and culture held with Bathukamma, Telangana's festival
  of flowers. Navadhara 2026 was held on 6 November 2026.
* **PRAGATHI — Project Expo** — the national project exhibition where students show what they have built
  (PRAGATHI 2.0 on 9 October 2026; featured on ETV News). Yantra Project Expo 2K25 was also held.
* **Swagatham — Freshers' Welcome** — the welcome festival for new students (16 October 2026).
* **Annual Sports Day** — held every year (the 24th Annual Sports Day was in 2026), alongside annual
  inter-university tournaments.
* **Hackathons** — overnight programming hackathons (8 PM to 8 AM) by the School of CS & AI and the Hackathon Club.
* **Other yearly events** — Orientation Day, SRU-MUN (Model United Nations), Bathukamma, the Alumni Meet and
  Convocation Day; Auto Expo, cultural evenings ("Campus Joy") and traditional celebrations.

## Sports and fitness
* Indoor and outdoor sports complex: basketball, cricket, football, badminton, tennis and more.
* Olympic-standard gym and fitness centres; annual inter-university tournaments.

## Student support and wellness
* 24/7 medical and emergency support with hospital tie-ups.
* Professional counsellors for stress and anxiety; peer support and student helplines.
* Career guidance: resume workshops and mock interviews.

## Hostel life
* Rooms: AC and non-AC, 3- or 4-sharing, with modern furniture, study desks and high-speed internet; separate
  hostels for boys and girls; recreational spaces; 24/7 surveillance.
* Services: laundry and housekeeping, RO drinking water on every floor, hot water 7–9 AM and 7–9 PM, 24x7 Wi-Fi,
  medical support with an ambulance, power backup.
* In-time: first-year students by 8:30 PM; senior students by 9:00 PM on weekdays and 9:30 PM at weekends.

## Food
* A 4,000-seat dining hall serving 4 meals a day: healthy, hygienic and nutritious, vegetarian and non-vegetarian.
* Mess timings: Breakfast 7:30–8:30 AM · Lunch 12:00–2:00 PM · Snacks 5:00–6:00 PM · Dinner 7:30–8:30 PM.
* Central Cafeteria 8:30 AM–6:00 PM; Tasty Treat, Meet and Eat and Bharat Foods kiosks 9:00 AM–7:00 PM.
* Meals are included in the hostel fee.
