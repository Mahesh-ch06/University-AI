# 42. Fee policy 2027-28: one policy file, one semester-wise engine (v4.16.0, 25 Nov 2026)

Source of truth: the official circular **SRU_Fee_Structure_and_Scholarship_Policy.pdf** ("SR University Fee
Circulation: Official Fee Structure & Merit Scholarship Matrix"). Everything below is built from it.

## 1. Where the fees live

| Piece | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fee policy (data) | `backend/app/sru_agent/fee_policy_2027_28.json` | The ONLY place a fee, slab, minimum or concession is written. Replaces `fees_2027_28.json` (deleted). |
| Fee engine | `backend/app/sru_agent/fees.py` (`fee_schedule`, `calculate_fee`) | The ONLY calculator. Chat, admission flow, cards and the website calculator all call it. |
| Eligibility | `backend/app/sru_agent/eligibility.py` | Reads `min_percent` from the policy. |
| Generated copies | `frontend/src/data/feeConfig.json`, `frontend/scripts/feeCases.json`, handbook pages 08 and 09 (fee block) | `cd backend && python scripts/export_fee_config.py` |
| Prompts | `backend/prompts/system.md`, `rag.md` | No amounts: a `{{FEE_POLICY}}` block is generated by `prompt_service.fee_policy_block()`. |

The language model never calculates a fee: the `calculate_fee` tool returns the schedule and the prompt and
tool description tell it to quote `semester_1`, `semester_2`, `first_year_total_payable` exactly.

## 2. Policy contents

* Tuition and yearly increase for every UG and PG programme (26 programme keys), unchanged figures from the
  circular, including B.Tech AI & Robotics (₹4,00,000, +₹12,000) and IEP (₹3,40,000, +₹10,000).
* Minimum marks: **55%** in Intermediate for B.Tech CSE and ECE (and all their specialisations), **50%** for every
  other UG programme, **50%** in the qualifying degree for PG with `min_percent_confirmed: false`
  ("subject to confirmation"). Meeting the minimum is never presented as guaranteed admission.
* Scholarship slabs 10/15/20/25/30/40% for Intermediate, CBSE/ICSE, JEE Main percentile, TG/AP EAPCET rank, IB and
  PG percentage. JEE 30% is 90-94.5 (as printed).
* Charges: enrollment ₹5,000; other fee ₹15,000; exam fee Year 1 ₹5,000; application form ₹1,100;
  specialisation ₹30,000 a year from Year 2; bus ₹30,000; Ph.D. ₹70,000; electricity deposit ₹5,000 non-AC /
  ₹10,000 AC. The caution deposit and laundry fee are **not** on the circular and were removed.
* 20 special-category concessions (new: Chancellor / Pro Chancellor Recommendation ₹10,000, marked
  `selectable: false`; "More than 50% Disability" replaces "50% or above").
* Terms: the yearly increase applies irrespective of scholarship; concessions apply per year after verification.

## 3. Calculation order (fees.fee_schedule)

1 resolve programme → 2 check minimum (`eligibility.assess`) → 3 base tuition → 4 scholarship
(best single score) → 5 deduct it → 6 add `(year − 1) × increment` → 7 concessions → 8 split **tuition only**
equally between the two semesters → 9 place concessions (cannot exceed that semester's tuition) →
10-11 place charges → 12 semester payables → 13 annual = Sem 1 + Sem 2 → 14 course = sum of all semesters →
15 application fee shown separately.

| Component | Placement |
|---|---|
| Tuition | split equally between Sem 1 and Sem 2 |
| Enrollment fee, Exam fee | Sem 1 of Year 1 only (one-time) |
| Other fee | whole, Sem 1 of every year (not split) |
| Specialisation fee | Sem 1 from Year 2, only when chosen and the programme is listed |
| Bus, hostel | only when chosen; `schedule.bus_semester` / `hostel_semester` |
| Electricity deposit | once, first hostel semester, AC or non-AC |
| Concessions | annual deduction, `schedule.concession_semester` |
| Application fee | outside every semester |

Worked example (from the circular): B.Tech CSE, Intermediate 95% → 30%: Sem 1 **₹1,44,000** (₹1,19,000 tuition +
₹5,000 + ₹15,000 + ₹5,000), Sem 2 **₹1,19,000**, first year **₹2,63,000**. The old "₹1,31,500 each semester" is gone.

## 4. Configurable assumptions (`schedule` in the policy)

`scholarship_continues_every_year` (true), `increment_rule` (flat after scholarship), `other_fee_every_year` (true),
`specialization_fee_programs` (["btech_cse"]), `bus_semester` (1), `hostel_semester` (1), `concession_semester` (1).
Change them only after the Admissions / Accounts office confirms; every flow follows.

## 5. Flags for administrative verification (`flags` in the result)

Marks below the programme minimum; the PG minimum (subject to confirmation); a JEE percentile between 94.5 and 95
(not covered by the circular, no scholarship applied); IB 30-37 (overlapping ranges, lower slab applied); more than
one concession selected (combination decided by the Admissions Office).

## 6. Knowledge and caches

* Handbook pages 08/09 regenerate from the policy; the vector index re-indexes automatically at start-up when the
  handbook changes (needs the embedding quota; retried on each start).
* `auto_seed.retire_obsolete_fee_knowledge()` runs at start-up: removes vector chunks carrying retired fee text
  (old FAQ fees, 60% cutoff, "970+" scholarship bands, caution deposit, stale fee-page chunks) and archives the
  retired "University FAQ — Fee" document (`status = inactive`, version `active = false`; nothing is deleted).
* RAG answer cache keys include a fingerprint of the policy and handbook: any fee change invalidates them.
* The FAQ DOCX (`data/SRU Agentic AI.docx`) answers were rewritten from the policy and indexed on 25 Nov 2026 with
  the additive mode, which deletes no database row:
  `python scripts/ingest_university_doc.py --file "data/SRU Agentic AI.docx" --add-missing`.
  * It indexes only missing or changed answers (matched by section and question) and never the retired Fee section.
  * A second run adds nothing.
  * The index now holds 74 FAQ answers (the 78 in the document minus the 4 Fee ones) and no retired fee text.
* The vector index was re-built from the current handbook once embeddings were available (187 handbook chunks).
* The start-up clean-up checks only the answer of a Q&A chunk for retired phrases, so a question such as "Is it 60%
  and above?" keeps its corrected answer.

3. Specialisation fee: which programmes (assumed B.Tech CSE only, when a specialisation is chosen)?
4. Payment schedule for bus, hostel and concessions (assumed Semester 1 of each year).
5. Scholarship continuation conditions in later years (assumed it continues).
6. JEE percentile 94.5-95 is not covered by any slab.
7. IB ranges overlap (30-33 / 30-37 / 35+).
8. B.Tech AI & Robotics and IEP minimum: 50% applied (not listed as CSE/ECE).
9. PG minimum 50%: to be confirmed.
10. Chancellor / Pro Chancellor Recommendation: granted on recommendation only (not offered in the calculator).
11. Can two concessions be combined, and with a merit scholarship?
